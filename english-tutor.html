04 — NEXT, THE KEY

Set up your free Gemini key

English tutor’s AI half runs on a Google Gemini key that you create yourself, so the requests go straight from your device to Google rather than through us. It is free, takes about two minutes, and never asks for a card. The app walks you through it, or watch it here once.

1 min 18 s · narrated · recorded in Daybook, another of our apps — the AI Studio part is identical

  1. Open the key page

    In English tutor, go to Settings → Gemini AI. The API key box at the top reads No key until you fill it.

  2. Go to AI Studio

    Open aistudio.google.com/apikey in your browser. Any Google account works.

  3. Accept the terms

    Tick the acknowledgement box and press Continue. No billing account is created and no card is requested.

  4. Create the key

    Press Create API key, keep the project it suggests, then press Create key.

  5. Copy it straight away

    Tap the copy icon next to the key. It is shown once — if you lose it, just make another.

  6. Paste it back and save

    Return to English tutor, paste the key into the box and press Save. The model list fills, which is how you know it works.

05 — WHAT IT DOES

Explained in your language, practised in English

Corrections you can learn from

Every change marked, the rule named — “for with a length of time, since with a starting point” — in your own language or in simple English.

Say it your way

Write what you mean in any language and get natural English back, with how it is built and other ways to say it.

Any page, tappable

Photograph a textbook, an article or a sign. Every word opens its meaning, and exercises on the page can be answered and checked.

Listen and speak

Record yourself or open any English audio for a timed transcript with translations. Hear every line in a British or American voice.

Your course book, planned

Import a PDF course book. It finds every unit, turns exercises into answer boxes, and lays out a study plan at your pace.

Words that stay, lessons that fit

Saved words come back as flashcards. Grammar lessons from A1 upward, and a review built from your own mistakes.

08 — PERMISSIONS

Bring your own AI key

The AI features aren't a service we run for you. You make your own free Gemini key and paste it into Settings, and the app calls Google directly from your device, as you. The key sits in the app's own settings storage and never reaches us. Your use of the API is between you and Google.

Works without a key offline

If you never add one, the offline half keeps working: the bundled dictionary, tap-a-word lookup, word forms, your saved list, the review cards, the PDF reader, text recognition and speech-to-text.

Camera pages & exercises

Opens only when you start it: photographing a page or an exercise, or scanning the pairing QR code from the desktop app. Recognition runs locally first, so where the device can read the text itself, the text is sent rather than the picture. Photos aren't uploaded anywhere else or added to any gallery.

Microphone local by default

Used only while you're recording, after you press record. Transcription runs on the device by default — Whisper through sherpa-onnx on Android, faster-whisper on Windows — and your audio stays there. Sending audio to Gemini is a fallback for when local transcription is off or unavailable.

Reading aloud your choice of voice

Only when you press a speaker button, and only the word or sentence being read. Piper voices run on the device; the system voice is your device's own; the Google and Azure voices send just that text to be spoken.

Google sign-in optional · drive.file

Optional, and there for one thing: syncing your word list through your own Drive with the narrow drive.file scope, which reaches nothing but the files the app created. No feature is locked behind signing in.

What it doesn't do

  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no crash reporting.
  • No accounts, no back end, no server of ours.
  • Your sentences, recordings, photos and word list never reach us.

The privacy policy goes feature by feature and says exactly what is and isn't sent to Gemini.

09 — QUESTIONS

Questions

Which languages can it explain in?
More than 95 are built in — Arabic, German, Spanish, Turkish, Chinese, Persian, Ukrainian, Hindi, French, Portuguese and many more — and you can type any other. Prefer to think in English? One switch puts every explanation in simple English.
Does it work offline?
Everything except the AI features: corrections, translation, exercise reading and progress reviews. Those need a connection and your own free key.
Do I pay for the AI features?
No. They run on your own free Gemini key, so there's no subscription.
