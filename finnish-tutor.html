02 — NEXT, THE KEY

Set up your free Gemini key

Finnish tutor’s AI half runs on a Google Gemini key that you create yourself, so the requests go straight from your device to Google rather than through us. It is free, takes about two minutes, and never asks for a card. Watch it once, or follow the steps.

1 min 18 s · narrated · recorded in Daybook, the other app — the AI Studio part is identical

  1. Open the key page

    In Finnish tutor, go to Settings → Gemini AI. The API key box at the top reads No key until you fill it.

  2. Go to AI Studio

    Open aistudio.google.com/apikey in your browser. Any Google account works.

  3. Accept the terms

    Tick the acknowledgement box and press Continue. No billing account is created and no card is requested.

  4. Create the key

    Press Create API key, keep the project it suggests, then press Create key.

  5. Copy it straight away

    Tap the copy icon next to the key. It is shown once — if you lose it, just make another.

  6. Paste it back and save

    Return to Finnish tutor, paste the key into the box and press Save. The pill flips to Key set and the model list fills, which is how you know it works.

07 — PERMISSIONS

Bring your own AI key

The AI features aren't a service we run for you. You make your own free Gemini key and paste it into Settings, and the app calls Google directly from your device, as you. The key sits in the app's own settings storage and never reaches us. Your use of the API is between you and Google.

Works without a key offline

If you never add one, the offline half keeps working: 174,932 bundled headwords, tap-a-word lookup, word forms, your saved list, the review cards, the PDF reader, text recognition and speech-to-text.

Camera pages & exercises

Opens only when you start it: photographing a page or an exercise, or scanning the pairing QR code from the desktop app. Recognition runs locally first, so where the device can read the text itself, the text is sent rather than the picture. Photos aren't uploaded anywhere else or added to any gallery.

Microphone local by default

Used only while you're recording, after you press record. Transcription runs on the device by default — Whisper through sherpa-onnx on Android, faster-whisper on Windows — and your audio stays there. Sending audio to Gemini is a fallback for when local transcription is off or unavailable.

Google sign-in optional · drive.file

Optional, and there for one thing: syncing your word list through your own Drive with the narrow drive.file scope, which reaches nothing but the files the app created. No feature is locked behind signing in.

What it doesn't do

  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no crash reporting.
  • No accounts, no back end, no server of ours.
  • Your sentences, recordings, photos and word list never reach us.
  • Nothing is sent in the background. A request happens when you press a button.

The privacy policy goes feature by feature and says exactly what is and isn't sent to Gemini.

08 — QUESTIONS

Questions

Does it work offline?
The offline half does: about 175,000 bundled headwords, tap-a-word lookup, word forms, your saved list, review cards, the PDF reader, text recognition and speech-to-text. Only the parts that need a language model — grammar reasoning, translation nuance, exercise reading and progress reviews — need a connection and your own key.
Do I pay for the AI features?
No. You bring your own free Gemini key, so there's nothing to buy from us.
Which platforms are supported?
Android, and Windows from the Microsoft Store, with a QR scan to pair the two so your settings carry across.
