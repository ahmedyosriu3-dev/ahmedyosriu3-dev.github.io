How to read this. A high score means the instrument currently looks good on five measurable properties — not that it will go up. The scout has no view on earnings, valuation, management or news, and it cannot know what you already believe. Its most useful column is diversifies me: that one is about your portfolio rather than about the market, and it is the hardest thing to eyeball yourself.

Screening is not an edge. Before you put money anywhere, add it to the watchlist and run strategy selection — if nothing clears the bar out-of-sample, the app will tell you so, and buy-and-hold may well be the better answer.
