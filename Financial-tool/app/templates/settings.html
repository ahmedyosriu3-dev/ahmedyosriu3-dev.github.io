⚪ Not configured. Five minutes of setup, then the desk reaches you wherever you are.
  1. In Telegram, message @BotFather and send /newbot. Give it any name. It replies with a token.
  2. Put that token in .env as TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN, and restart the app.
  3. Send your new bot any message — say hello.
  4. Press the button below; it reads your chat id off that message.
  5. Put the id in .env as TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID and restart once more.
