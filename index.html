03 — DAYBOOK

Daybook Android · Desktop · Free

A personal diary, task and activity log that stays on your device.

Daybook records your day — tasks, notes, activities, places, birthdays and holidays — in a database on your own phone or computer. It has no account system and no server of its own. Connecting a Google account is optional, and every feature that leaves the device is off until you switch it on.

Tasks & subtasks

Separate work and personal lists, with due dates, recurrence and a full completion history.

Notes

Written notes with sketches and photos attached, kept alongside the day they belong to.

Activity log

A running record of what you did and where — filled in by hand, or written for you from saved places.

Places

Save somewhere you go often and Daybook can log arrivals and departures for you.

Dates that matter

Birthdays, custom recurring events, and public holidays you choose to import.

AI assistant

An optional assistant tab powered by Google Gemini, using an API key you supply yourself.

Why Daybook asks for a Google account

Signing in is entirely optional — Daybook works fully without it. It exists so the app can back up to your Drive and, on desktop, write your dated tasks into your calendar. Daybook has no login of its own, so there is nowhere else for that data to go. Here is every permission the app requests and what it is used for.

Google Drive backup drive.file

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

Used to upload a copy of Daybook's own database, plus images attached to your notes, so you can restore on a new device or keep two devices in step. This scope grants access only to files Daybook itself created — the app cannot see, list or open anything else already in your Drive. The backup sits in your own Drive under your own account; the developer has no access to it.

Google Calendar sync calendar

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar

Used so that tasks you give a date appear as events in your own Google Calendar, visible from any device signed into that account. Only the tasks and events you choose to sync are written — never your notes, your activity log, or anything else in the app.

This route is offered in Daybook's desktop build. On Android there is a second, separate option that writes to your device's own calendar app using the ordinary OS calendar permission and no Google account at all; that is the route the Google Play version uses today.

Your email address and basic profile openid · email · profile

Read once after sign-in purely so the app can show you which account is currently connected. Daybook does not read your contacts, your mail, or any other part of the account.

Sign-in tokens are stored on your device alongside the database and are used only for the calls described above. You can withdraw Daybook's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What Daybook does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no data brokers — none are built into the app.
  • No account to create, and no Daybook server for your entries to sit on.
  • Your notes, tasks and logs are never transmitted to the developer.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on the specific feature that sends it.

The privacy policy goes through this feature by feature — including location, microphone, camera and the AI assistant — and says plainly where each one sends data, or that it sends none.

04 — FINNISH TUTOR

Finnish tutor Android · Windows · Free

Correct your Finnish, read any page, and keep every word you meet.

A study companion for anyone learning Finnish, built to do as much as possible on your own device and to explain rather than just answer. Type a sentence and it comes back with every change marked, a plain-English reason for each one, and every word broken down into its forms.

Write it, see what changed

Every correction marked, with the reason — “motion into requires the illative case” — and full case tables underneath.

Photograph a page

Text recognition runs on the device and every word on the photo becomes a tap target with its dictionary entry.

Say it, read it back

Record yourself speaking Finnish and have it transcribed — fully on-device on Android.

Work through a book

Import a PDF course book, turn pages, write on them with a pen, and practise the tasks a page prints.

Every word kept

Words save from anywhere into one list with their forms filled in, reviewed as spaced-repetition cards, exportable as CSV.

Built from your mistakes

Each correction becomes a topic, so the grammar you keep getting wrong turns into your own curriculum.

Bring your own AI key

The AI features are not a service the developer runs on your behalf. You create your own free Google Gemini API key and paste it into Settings; the app then calls Google's API directly from your device, as you. The key is stored in the app's own settings storage on your device and is never transmitted to the developer — there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to. Your use of the Gemini API is governed by your own agreement with Google.

Works without a key offline

If you never add a key, the offline half of the app keeps working: about 174,932 bundled Finnish headwords, tap-a-word lookup, word forms, your saved list, the review cards, the PDF reader, on-device text recognition and on-device speech-to-text.

Camera page & exercise photos

Opened only when you start it: photographing a page or an exercise, or scanning the pairing QR code from the desktop app. Recognition runs locally first, so where the device can read the text itself, the extracted text is sent rather than the picture. Photographs are not uploaded anywhere else and are not added to any gallery.

Microphone on-device by default

Used only while you are recording, after you press record. On-device transcription is the default path — Whisper through sherpa-onnx on Android, faster-whisper on Windows — and in that mode your audio never leaves the device. Sending audio to Gemini is only a fallback when local transcription is switched off or unavailable.

Google sign-in optional · drive.file

Entirely optional, and there for one purpose: syncing your word list through your own Google Drive using the narrow drive.file scope, which can touch nothing but the files the app itself created. Nothing in the app is gated behind signing in.

What Finnish tutor does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no crash reporting.
  • No accounts, no back end, and no server operated by the developer.
  • The developer never receives your sentences, recordings, photographs or word list.
  • Nothing is sent in the background — a request happens when you press a button, and at no other time.

The privacy policy goes feature by feature and says exactly what is and is not sent to Google's Gemini API.

05 — QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Are the apps free?
Yes. Daybook and Finnish tutor are both free to use, with no subscription for the features described on this page. Finnish tutor's AI features run on your own free Google Gemini API key, so there is nothing to buy from MAIA Architech.
Where is my data stored?
In a database inside the app's own storage on your phone or computer — for Daybook, a single local SQLite database that other apps on the device cannot read. There is no MAIA Architech server and no account system, so there is nowhere else for your entries to go.
Does Daybook work without a Google account?
Yes, fully. Signing in is optional and only enables two things: backup to your own Google Drive, and writing dated tasks into your own Google Calendar. You can withdraw access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
Do the apps show ads or track me?
No. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no crash reporting built into either app, and your notes, tasks, recordings and word lists are never transmitted to the developer.
Does Finnish tutor work offline?
The offline half does: about 175,000 bundled Finnish headwords, tap-a-word lookup, word forms, your saved list, review cards, the PDF reader, on-device text recognition and on-device speech-to-text. Only the features that need a large language model — grammar reasoning, translation nuance, exercise reading and progress reviews — require a connection and your own Gemini API key.
Which platforms are supported?
Daybook runs on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux. Finnish tutor runs on Android and Windows, with one-scan QR pairing between them.
