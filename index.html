02 — QUESTIONS

Questions

How do I install them?
Both are in closed testing on Google Play, so they don't show up in search yet. Join the tester group for the app, accept the invitation, and the listing opens for your account. Use the account your phone's Play Store is signed in to. Steps and links are on the Daybook and Finnish tutor pages.
What do they cost?
The browser and Android versions are free. The Windows builds are $2.69 each, sold on the Microsoft Store. There's no subscription either way: the AI features run on your own free Gemini key.
Where is my data kept?
In the app's own storage on your phone or computer. There's no MAIA Architech server and no account to create.
Do the apps show ads or track me?
No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no crash reporting.
Which platforms are supported?
Android and Windows for both apps, plus Daybook in the browser. The Windows builds are on the Microsoft Store, and Finnish tutor pairs phone and desktop with a QR scan.
